⚠️ Sandbagging signal — who is lowballing their forecast

Each open deal gets a sandbag gap = the probability its sales stage implies, minus the probability the rep actually assigned. A consistently positive gap means a rep is parking advanced deals at artificially low odds — they look conservative now, then "beat" their number when the deals land. Bars show average gap per rep; reps over the amber line warrant a forecast review.

Rep leaderboard

Deal-level detail

Every deal matching the current filters — your drill-down surface. Click any column header to sort; narrow it with the filters above or by clicking a chart.

Methodology. Bookings are bucketed by Close Date. Weighted forecast = Σ(Deal Amount × Probability). Stage benchmarks used for sandbag detection — Prospecting 10%, Discovery 25%, Qualification 40%, Proposal 60%, Negotiation 80%, Verbal Commit 90%. A deal is flagged when its assigned probability is ≥15 points below its stage benchmark, or when an advanced-stage deal sits in a Pipeline/Omitted forecast category.
